# Project Brief

**Project name:** Volunteer Impact Coordinator

**Sustainability theme:** Technology for Sustainable Futures — People (social sustainability, community wellbeing)

## Purpose

Community organisations rely heavily on volunteers, but they often struggle to coordinate schedules, keep accurate records of contributions, and demonstrate the real social impact of their work to funders and members. The Volunteer Impact Coordinator is a JavaFX desktop application that helps small to medium community organisations manage volunteers, schedule events, track hours contributed, and generate reports that clearly show engagement and outcomes.

## Target Users

- Volunteer coordinators at community organisations, charities, and not-for-profits
- Individual volunteers who want to track their own contributions and view upcoming events

## Sustainability Relevance

The app supports social sustainability by strengthening community organisations. Better coordination means less coordinator burnout, higher volunteer retention, and stronger evidence of social impact when applying for grants or renewing funding. All of these directly contribute to the long-term wellbeing of the community programs volunteers support.

## Key Features

- User accounts with two roles: coordinator and volunteer
- Volunteer profile management including areas of interest and availability
- Event and activity scheduling
- Volunteer sign-up and attendance tracking
- Hours logging with approval workflow
- Reporting dashboard with engagement statistics and impact summaries
- Persistent data storage in a local SQLite database

## Measurable Outcomes

- Time saved per week by coordinators compared to manual spreadsheet tracking
- Number of volunteer hours accurately recorded per month
- Retention rate of volunteers over a semester of use
- Number of impact reports generated for stakeholders
